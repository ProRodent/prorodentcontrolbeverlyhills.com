Every city below is a real, separately incorporated municipality with its own city government, matched to its own ZIP code or codes from our coverage data. Beverly Hills sits inside the dense Los Angeles metro area, where the postal service assigns vanity city names to many neighborhoods and unincorporated communities that are not their own town. We only build a page for a place when it is an actual city. A name you may recognize from an address label, such as Studio City or Sherman Oaks, will not appear on this list, because it names a district inside the City of Los Angeles rather than a separate municipality.

Los Angeles itself is the exception on this list only because of its size. It borders Beverly Hills on every side and, in coverage terms, is one enormous city with dozens of ZIP codes. We list its five closest verified ZIPs to Beverly Hills rather than all of them, and the same approach applies to a few other large cities on this list.

If your city is not listed, call anyway. We can usually confirm coverage on the spot, and every visit still starts with the same rodent inspection whether you are a mile from Beverly Hills or fifteen.

Distances below are approximate straight-line miles from Beverly Hills, not drive time. Pick your city from the list, or use the map to find the closest match.

Questions about our coverage area

How do I know if my ZIP code is covered?

Find your city in the list below. Each city's page names its own ZIP code or codes. If you do not see your city, call and we will check.

Do you charge more to travel to farther cities?

We quote after an inspection, the same as for Beverly Hills itself. Distance is one factor among several, never a flat surcharge we can quote over the phone.

Is the emergency line the same number for every city?

Yes. One line covers Beverly Hills and every city on this list, open 24/7.

Why isn't a place I know by name, like Studio City or Sherman Oaks, on this list?

Those are postal names for neighborhoods inside the City of Los Angeles, not their own city. We serve those addresses under the Los Angeles page and coverage ZIPs, but we only build a dedicated page for a place with its own municipal government.

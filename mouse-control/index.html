What a mouse control visit covers.

House mice are small enough to fit through a gap about the width of a pencil, and Beverly Hills homes give them plenty of those gaps. A visit starts with a walk-through of the attic access, garage door seals, dryer vents and any utility penetrations where pipes or wiring enter the house. We are looking for grease marks, droppings and chewed material that tell us where the animals are actually moving, not just where you happen to hear them.

Once we know the route, we place traps and tamper-resistant stations along it rather than scattering them through the house. Mice rarely travel far from a food source, so a kitchen or pantry infestation usually traces back to a specific cabinet base, an appliance gap or a laundry room wall. Our rodent inspection service is the same first step if you would rather start with a diagnosis before committing to a full visit.

Why Beverly Hills homes see mouse activity.

Older homes in the Flats, south of Sunset, have decades of settling behind their stucco and siding, and that settling opens small gaps at sills, chimney flashing and where additions meet the original structure. Newer construction and remodeled hillside homes near Trousdale Estates are not immune either, since utility penetrations from a remodel are a common unsealed entry point even in a recently finished house.

Southern California's dry season pushes mice toward any reliable water source, and a kitchen, laundry room or garage with a slow leak becomes an attractive target. Garages used for storage give mice undisturbed nesting space in boxes, and a car that sits for a few days without being driven can end up with a mouse nest under the hood, which is where our vehicle and car rodent removal service comes in.

How the visit is run.

We inspect first, then treat, then seal, then follow up. That order matters because trapping without sealing just resets the clock: new mice move in through the same gap once the current ones are gone. On a first visit we explain what we found, where traps are going, and what to expect over the next week or two.

  • Traps and stations placed along confirmed travel routes, not scattered at random.
  • Entry points documented with photos before any sealing work begins.
  • Tamper-resistant placements kept out of reach of children and pets.
  • A follow-up visit scheduled to check traps and confirm the seal is holding.

Keeping mice from coming back.

Sealing is the part of the job that actually stops the problem long term. We use hardware cloth, sheet metal and mortar rather than foam alone, because foam does not hold up against sustained chewing. Roofline gaps, vent screens and garage door bottom seals are the most common fixes on Beverly Hills homes, and our rodent exclusion service covers this work in more depth when a property needs a broader sealing pass rather than a spot fix.

If the activity sounds more like burrowing outdoors or larger droppings than a mouse would leave, it may be a rat problem instead. The approach differs enough that it is worth mentioning on the call so we bring the right equipment the first time.

What to expect on the day of the visit.

A technician arrives with traps, tamper-resistant stations and sealing material already on hand, since most Beverly Hills mouse calls need some combination of all three. The visit starts with the walk-through described above, then moves straight into placement once the likely routes are confirmed. We talk you through what we found before starting any work, including anywhere access was limited, such as a locked crawl space or a stored item we could not safely move ourselves.

Most homeowners want to know how soon the activity will stop. A single mouse or a small, contained kitchen problem often quiets down within a few days of trapping. A larger infestation tied to an open attic gap, common on older Flats homes that have never had exclusion work done, takes longer since new mice can keep arriving until the gap itself is closed. Either way, we set expectations on the first visit rather than leaving you guessing.

Condos, guest houses and detached structures.

Beverly Hills has a mix of single-family homes, condominiums and properties with a detached guest house or pool house, and each needs a slightly different plan. A condo unit often traces activity back to a shared wall or a utility chase serving several units, so we note when a building-wide issue looks likely rather than treating it as isolated to one unit. A detached guest house or pool house gets the same full inspection as the main house, since it can hold an entirely separate nest that has nothing to do with what is happening in the primary residence.

Questions about mouse control

How can I tell if I have mice or rats?

Check droppings and gaps. Mouse droppings are small and pointed, roughly 1/8 to 1/4 inch, and mice fit through openings about the width of a pencil. Rat droppings are larger and blunt, and rats tend to travel outdoors through trees and hedges.

How do mice get into a sealed-looking house?

A mouse only needs a gap about 1/4 inch wide, which is smaller than most people expect. Vent screens, utility penetrations and garage door seals are common overlooked entry points even on well-maintained homes.

Will one visit solve the problem?

Trapping usually removes the active mice within the first visit or two, but sealing the entry point is what prevents new mice from moving in. We schedule a follow-up to confirm both parts are working.

Is the treatment safe around pets and children?

We use tamper-resistant bait stations and place everything out of reach of children, pets and wildlife, and we tell you exactly what was placed and where before we leave.

Do you handle mice in a garage or detached structure?

Yes, we treat every structure on the property, not just the main house, since a garage or guest house can hold its own separate nest.

What if I hear noises but never see a mouse?

That is common. Mice are mostly active at night and stay hidden during the day, so noise in the walls or attic without a sighting still points to an active problem worth inspecting.
